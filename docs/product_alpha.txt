Product: Alpha Analytics Dashboard
Version: 3.2
Launch Date: March 2024

Overview:
Alpha is Uber's internal analytics dashboard used by operations teams to monitor ride demand, 
driver supply, and pricing efficiency across cities.

Key Features:
- Real-time surge pricing visualization
- City-level heat maps for supply/demand imbalance
- Driver earnings breakdown by time of day
- Weekly cohort retention reports

Target Users: City operations managers, pricing analysts, regional GMs

Known Issues:
- Dashboard loads slowly for cities with >500k weekly trips
- Export to CSV currently limited to 90-day windows

Pricing: Internal tool, no external pricing
