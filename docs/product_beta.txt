Product: Beta Driver App (Next Gen)
Version: 5.0 (Beta)
Launch Date: Q3 2024

Overview:
Beta Driver is the redesigned driver-facing mobile app with improved navigation, 
earnings visibility, and in-app support.

Key Features:
- Predictive destination suggestions based on time and location
- Earnings goal tracker with daily/weekly targets
- One-tap in-app support with 2-minute response SLA
- Background noise cancellation for passenger calls

Target Users: Uber driver-partners globally

Known Issues:
- Predictive suggestions accuracy drops in suburban zones
- Earnings tracker does not yet support UberEats deliveries

Pricing: Free for all driver-partners
